The App itself does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, photos or contacts.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically:

3. Data stored on your device

Your game progress, unlocked levels and settings are stored locally on your device. If you have Android Backup enabled, this data may be backed up to your own Google account so it can be restored after reinstalling. We do not have access to that backup.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information collected by the third-party services above is handled according to their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising choices

You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads. Purchasing "Remove Ads" disables ads in the App.

The App is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
